
=== PERSONAL LOANS ===

A personal loan is an unsecured loan provided by banks and financial institutions to individuals for personal use such as medical emergencies, travel, wedding expenses, education, or home renovation.

Eligibility for Personal Loan:
- Age: 21 to 60 years (salaried), 25 to 65 years (self-employed)
- Minimum monthly income: Rs. 25,000 for salaried individuals
- Minimum business income: Rs. 5 lakhs per annum for self-employed
- Credit score: 700 or above recommended
- Employment: Minimum 2 years of work experience, 1 year with current employer
- Nationality: Indian resident

Personal Loan Interest Rates:
- Standard interest rate: 10.5% to 24% per annum depending on credit profile
- Processing fee: 1% to 3% of loan amount
- Prepayment charges: 2% to 5% of outstanding principal
- No prepayment allowed within first 6 months

Personal Loan Amount and Tenure:
- Minimum loan amount: Rs. 50,000
- Maximum loan amount: Rs. 40 lakhs
- Loan tenure: 1 year to 5 years (12 to 60 months)
- EMI calculated based on reducing balance method

Documents Required for Personal Loan:
- Identity proof: Aadhaar card, PAN card, Passport, Voter ID
- Address proof: Utility bills, Rental agreement, Passport
- Income proof: Last 3 months salary slips, Form 16, ITR for last 2 years
- Bank statements: Last 6 months bank account statements
- Employment proof: Appointment letter, employee ID

Personal Loan Application Process:
1. Apply online or visit branch
2. Submit required documents
3. Bank verifies documents and credit score
4. Loan approval within 24 to 72 hours
5. Amount disbursed to bank account within 2 to 5 working days

=== HOME LOANS ===

A home loan is a secured loan provided to purchase, construct, or renovate a residential property. The property acts as collateral until the loan is fully repaid.

Home Loan Types:
- Home Purchase Loan: For buying a ready-to-move or under-construction property
- Home Construction Loan: For constructing a new house on owned land
- Home Improvement Loan: For renovation, repair, or extension of existing home
- Home Extension Loan: For adding additional space to existing property
- Balance Transfer Loan: Transfer existing home loan to another lender at lower rate
- NRI Home Loan: For Non-Resident Indians to purchase property in India

Home Loan Eligibility:
- Age: 18 to 70 years (loan must be repaid before age 70)
- Minimum income: Rs. 25,000 per month for salaried
- Credit score: 750 or above recommended for best rates
- Loan-to-Value (LTV) ratio: Up to 90% for loans up to Rs. 30 lakhs, 80% for Rs. 30-75 lakhs, 75% for above Rs. 75 lakhs

Home Loan Interest Rates:
- Floating interest rate: 8.5% to 11% per annum (linked to REPO rate)
- Fixed interest rate: 9% to 12% per annum for first 2-3 years
- Women borrowers get 0.05% concession on interest rate
- PMAY subsidy available for eligible first-time homebuyers

Home Loan Documentation:
- KYC documents: PAN, Aadhaar, Passport
- Income documents: Salary slips, ITR, Form 16, bank statements
- Property documents: Sale agreement, NOC from builder, property tax receipts
- Legal documents: Title deed, encumbrance certificate

Home Loan Repayment:
- Maximum tenure: 30 years
- Prepayment of floating rate loan: No penalty charges
- Prepayment of fixed rate loan: 2% charges on prepaid amount
- Part prepayment allowed after 6 months of loan disbursement

=== CREDIT CARDS ===

A credit card is a payment card that allows cardholders to borrow funds to pay for goods and services, with the obligation to repay the borrowed amount along with applicable interest.

Types of Credit Cards:
- Classic/Basic Card: Entry-level card with basic features, low credit limit
- Silver Card: Mid-range card with reward points and basic offers
- Gold Card: Premium card with higher credit limit, travel benefits, and lounge access
- Platinum Card: High-end card with concierge services and premium rewards
- Super Premium Card: Invite-only cards with exclusive benefits for HNI customers
- Co-branded Cards: Partnership cards with airlines, retailers, or OTT platforms
- Business Credit Cards: Designed for business expenses with expense management features

Credit Card Fees and Charges:
- Annual fee: Rs. 0 to Rs. 10,000 depending on card type
- Joining fee: Rs. 0 to Rs. 5,000 (often waived on spending milestone)
- Annual fee waiver: Typically on spending Rs. 1 to 2 lakhs per year
- Late payment fee: Rs. 100 to Rs. 1,300 depending on outstanding amount
- Over-limit fee: 2.5% of over-limit amount, minimum Rs. 500
- Cash advance fee: 2.5% to 3% of withdrawn amount, minimum Rs. 500
- Foreign transaction fee: 1.5% to 3.5% of transaction amount
- Duplicate statement fee: Rs. 100 per statement

Credit Card Interest Rates:
- Monthly interest rate: 2.5% to 3.75% per month
- Annual percentage rate: 30% to 45% per annum
- Interest-free period: 20 to 50 days from transaction date
- Cash advances: Interest charged from day of withdrawal, no interest-free period

Credit Card Reward Points:
- Earn rate: 1 to 10 reward points per Rs. 100 spent
- Accelerated points: 2x to 10x points on select categories
- Redemption: Against statement credit, flights, hotels, merchandise, Amazon/Flipkart vouchers
- Expiry: Points valid for 2 to 3 years from date of earn

Credit Card Safety and Security:
- Set transaction PIN for all card-present transactions
- Enable/disable international transactions as required
- Set spending limits on card
- OTP mandatory for all online transactions (2FA)
- Report lost/stolen card immediately by calling customer care
- Block card temporarily using mobile banking app
- Fraud liability protection: Zero liability if reported within 3 days

Credit Limit:
- Based on income, credit score, existing obligations
- Credit utilization ratio should be below 30% for good credit score
- Credit limit increase request can be made after 6 months of good usage

=== SAVINGS ACCOUNTS ===

A savings account is a deposit account held at a bank that earns interest on the deposited funds. It is the most common type of bank account for individuals.

Types of Savings Accounts:
- Regular Savings Account: Standard account with basic features
- Zero Balance Savings Account (Basic Savings Account): No minimum balance required
- Senior Citizen Savings Account: Higher interest rates for customers above 60 years
- Women's Savings Account: Special offers, insurance benefits for women
- Kids/Minor Savings Account: For children below 18, operated by guardian
- Premium/Privilege Savings Account: Higher balance requirement, premium benefits
- Salary Account: Corporate salary account with zero balance facility
- NRI Savings Account (NRE/NRO): For Non-Resident Indians

Savings Account Interest Rates:
- Standard rate: 2.7% to 7% per annum depending on balance and bank
- Interest calculated daily on closing balance
- Interest credited quarterly (March, June, September, December)
- Senior citizens get additional 0.25% to 0.50% on some accounts

Minimum Balance Requirements:
- Metro/Urban branches: Rs. 5,000 to Rs. 10,000
- Semi-urban branches: Rs. 2,000 to Rs. 5,000
- Rural branches: Rs. 500 to Rs. 1,000
- Zero balance accounts: No minimum balance
- Penalty for non-maintenance: Rs. 100 to Rs. 750 per month

Savings Account Features:
- Free debit card (Visa/Mastercard/RuPay)
- Net banking and mobile banking access
- UPI transactions via linked account
- Free IMPS, NEFT, RTGS through mobile/net banking
- Passbook and monthly e-statement facility
- ATM cash withdrawal: 5 free transactions per month at own bank ATMs
- Other bank ATMs: 3 free transactions per month in metro cities

Account Opening:
- Visit branch or apply online
- Submit KYC documents (Aadhaar + PAN mandatory)
- Initial deposit as per account type
- Account activated within 24-48 hours
- Debit card delivered within 7-10 working days

=== FIXED DEPOSITS ===

A Fixed Deposit (FD) is a financial instrument provided by banks offering investors a higher rate of interest than a regular savings account, until the given maturity date.

Fixed Deposit Interest Rates:
- General public: 3% to 7.5% per annum depending on tenure
- Senior citizens: Additional 0.25% to 0.75% over general rates
- Super senior citizens (above 80 years): Additional 0.10% to 0.25% extra
- Highest rates typically for 1 to 3 year tenure

Fixed Deposit Tenure Options:
- Minimum tenure: 7 days
- Maximum tenure: 10 years
- Short-term: 7 days to 1 year
- Medium-term: 1 year to 5 years
- Long-term: 5 years to 10 years

Fixed Deposit Features:
- Guaranteed returns with zero market risk
- Auto-renewal facility available
- Nomination facility available
- Loan against FD: Up to 90% of FD amount at FD rate + 1-2%
- Tax-saving FD: 5-year lock-in, eligible for Section 80C deduction up to Rs. 1.5 lakhs
- Sweep-in FD: Automatic FD from savings account excess amount

Premature Withdrawal:
- Allowed with penalty of 0.5% to 1% on applicable rate
- Some banks offer penalty-free premature withdrawal after specific lock-in
- Tax-saving FD cannot be withdrawn before 5 years
- Partial premature withdrawal not allowed (full FD must be broken)

TDS on Fixed Deposits:
- TDS deducted at 10% if interest exceeds Rs. 40,000 per year (Rs. 50,000 for senior citizens)
- Submit Form 15G (for non-senior citizens below tax bracket) or Form 15H (for senior citizens) to avoid TDS
- TDS certificate (Form 16A) issued quarterly

=== CURRENT ACCOUNTS ===

A current account is a deposit account for business entities and individuals with high transaction volumes. It allows unlimited transactions without interest on the deposited amount.

Current Account Features:
- Unlimited transactions (no restriction on number of deposits/withdrawals)
- Overdraft facility available (credit line against account)
- Multi-city cheque book facility
- No interest earned on deposited amount
- Free RTGS, NEFT, and IMPS transactions (up to certain limits)
- Dedicated relationship manager for premium accounts
- Cash deposit and withdrawal at any branch

Current Account Types:
- Regular Current Account: For small businesses and proprietorships
- Premium Current Account: For mid-size businesses with higher transaction limits
- Trade Current Account: For import/export businesses with forex facilities
- Escrow Account: For real estate developers and specific business arrangements
- Nodal Account: For payment aggregators and marketplace platforms

Minimum Balance Requirement:
- Metro cities: Rs. 25,000 to Rs. 1,00,000
- Other cities: Rs. 10,000 to Rs. 50,000
- Penalty: Rs. 500 to Rs. 2,500 per month for non-maintenance

Overdraft Facility:
- Based on account turnover and credit history
- Typically 1x to 3x of monthly average balance
- Interest rate: 12% to 18% per annum on utilized amount
- Overdraft limit reviewed annually

=== DIGITAL BANKING AND NET BANKING ===

Net Banking / Internet Banking:
- Secure online portal to access bank accounts 24/7
- Fund transfers: IMPS (instant), NEFT (2 hours), RTGS (same day, above Rs. 2 lakhs)
- Bill payments: Electricity, gas, water, mobile recharge, DTH, insurance
- Fixed deposit creation and premature closure
- Loan account management and EMI payment
- Tax payment (direct tax, GST)
- Cheque book request and stop payment

Mobile Banking App Features:
- UPI payments using registered mobile number
- QR code-based payments
- Account balance and mini statement
- Fund transfer to saved and new beneficiaries
- Credit card management (block/unblock, limit change, EMI conversion)
- Loan statements and EMI schedule
- Demat account integration
- Investment in mutual funds and FDs

UPI (Unified Payments Interface):
- Instant 24/7 money transfer using UPI ID, mobile number, or QR code
- Transaction limit: Rs. 1 lakh per transaction (Rs. 2 lakhs for certain categories)
- Linked to savings/current account
- No additional charges for UPI transactions
- Supports multiple bank accounts in single UPI app

Security Features in Digital Banking:
- Two-factor authentication (OTP + password)
- Biometric login (fingerprint/face ID) in mobile app
- Transaction-level OTP verification
- Daily transaction limits configurable by customer
- Real-time SMS/email alerts for all transactions
- Session timeout after inactivity
- 128-bit SSL encryption for all web transactions

=== ACCOUNT OPENING AND KYC ===

KYC (Know Your Customer) Requirements:
- Officially Valid Documents (OVD) for identity: Aadhaar card, PAN card, Passport, Voter ID, Driving License
- Address proof: Aadhaar card, Passport, Utility bills (not older than 3 months), Bank statement
- PAN card mandatory for accounts with annual transactions above Rs. 50,000
- Video KYC available for online account opening (V-CIP process)

Account Opening Process:
1. Visit branch with original documents or apply online
2. Fill account opening form (physical or digital)
3. Submit KYC documents (originals for verification, copies retained)
4. Initial deposit (as per account type)
5. Account number generated immediately
6. Welcome kit (debit card, passbook, cheque book) delivered in 7-10 days

Re-KYC Requirements:
- Periodic KYC update required every 2 years for high-risk customers
- Every 8-10 years for low-risk customers
- Update required when there is a change in address, name, or contact details
- Failure to complete re-KYC results in account freeze for debit transactions

Nomination Facility:
- Nominee can be added at account opening or later
- Maximum 1 nominee per account (joint holders can have separate nominees)
- Nominee receives account balance on death of account holder
- Nomination update can be done online or at branch

=== LOANS AND CREDIT - GENERAL POLICIES ===

Credit Score and Its Impact:
- CIBIL score range: 300 to 900
- Score above 750: Excellent - best interest rates, easy approval
- Score 650 to 750: Good - standard rates, mostly approved
- Score 550 to 650: Fair - higher rates, limited loan amounts
- Score below 550: Poor - loan rejection likely
- Factors affecting CIBIL: Payment history (35%), Credit utilization (30%), Credit history length (15%), Credit mix (10%), New credit inquiries (10%)

EMI Calculation:
- EMI = [P × R × (1+R)^N] / [(1+R)^N - 1]
- P = Principal amount, R = Monthly interest rate, N = Number of months
- Example: Rs. 5 lakh loan at 12% p.a. for 5 years = EMI of Rs. 11,122

Prepayment and Foreclosure:
- Floating rate loans: No prepayment penalty as per RBI guidelines
- Fixed rate loans: Prepayment penalty of 2% to 4% of outstanding amount
- Foreclosure statement can be obtained from branch or net banking
- NOC (No Objection Certificate) issued within 15 days of full repayment

Loan Against Securities:
- Loan against FD: Up to 90% of FD value, interest rate = FD rate + 2%
- Loan against shares: Up to 50% of share value (as per RBI guidelines)
- Loan against mutual funds: Up to 50% of current NAV
- Loan against insurance policy: Up to 90% of surrender value

=== CUSTOMER SUPPORT AND GRIEVANCE REDRESSAL ===

Customer Support Channels:
- 24/7 toll-free helpline: Available for all banking queries and emergency card blocking
- Branch visit: Monday to Friday 10 AM to 4 PM, Saturday 10 AM to 2 PM
- Email support: Response within 2 business days
- Live chat on mobile app and website: Available 8 AM to 10 PM
- Social media support: Twitter, Facebook (response within 4 hours on working days)

Complaint Registration:
- Register complaint online via net banking portal
- Call customer care and request complaint registration
- Visit nearest branch and fill complaint form
- Written complaint to Branch Manager
- Complaint reference number provided for tracking

Grievance Redressal Timeline:
- Complaints resolved within 30 days as per RBI guidelines
- Priority complaints (fraud, unauthorized transactions): Resolved within 7 days
- ATM transaction failure: Resolved within 7 working days, auto-credit if delayed
- NEFT/RTGS failures: Resolved within 2 hours (same day) or next working day

Banking Ombudsman:
- Approach Banking Ombudsman if complaint not resolved within 30 days
- No complaint pending beyond 1 year at first tier
- Available in all major cities
- Online complaint at cms.rbi.org.in
- No fee for filing complaint with Banking Ombudsman

=== RBI GUIDELINES AND BANKING REGULATIONS ===

Key RBI Guidelines for Customers:
- All banks must resolve ATM transaction failure within 7 working days with auto-compensation
- Zero liability for unauthorized transactions if reported immediately
- Limited liability (max Rs. 10,000) if reported within 4-7 days
- Banks cannot charge for NEFT/RTGS via internet banking
- Minimum 4 free ATM transactions per month from any bank ATM
- Banks must provide basic savings account with zero balance requirement

Deposit Insurance:
- All bank deposits insured by DICGC (Deposit Insurance and Credit Guarantee Corporation)
- Insurance coverage: Up to Rs. 5 lakhs per depositor per bank
- Covers savings, current, fixed, and recurring deposits
- Does not cover deposits in cooperative societies (non-scheduled)

Banking Hours and Holidays:
- Regular banking hours: 10 AM to 4 PM on weekdays
- Second and fourth Saturdays: Bank holiday
- All Sundays: Bank holiday
- National holidays: Banks remain closed
- ATMs, mobile banking, and net banking available 24/7

=== BANKING SECURITY AND FRAUD PREVENTION ===

Common Banking Frauds and Prevention:
- Phishing: Never click on suspicious links in emails or SMS claiming to be from bank
- Vishing: Bank never asks for OTP, card number, or password over phone
- SIM Swap Fraud: Monitor for sudden loss of mobile network connectivity
- ATM Skimming: Check ATM card slot for tampering before inserting card
- Fake UPI Requests: Never scan QR codes or approve requests to receive money

What to Do If Fraud Occurs:
1. Immediately block card via mobile app or call customer care
2. Change net banking and mobile banking password
3. Report to bank within 3 days for zero liability protection
4. File police complaint and obtain FIR copy
5. File complaint at cybercrime.gov.in
6. Keep all transaction records and communication as evidence

Safe Banking Practices:
- Never share OTP, PIN, or password with anyone including bank employees
- Register mobile number with bank for real-time alerts
- Regularly check account statements for unauthorized transactions
- Use strong, unique passwords for net banking
- Enable two-factor authentication wherever available
- Avoid using public Wi-Fi for banking transactions
- Regularly update mobile banking app

=== RECURRING DEPOSITS ===

A Recurring Deposit (RD) is a type of term deposit where a fixed amount is deposited every month for a predetermined period and earns interest at a fixed rate.

RD Features:
- Monthly deposit amount: Rs. 100 onwards
- Tenure: 6 months to 10 years
- Interest rate: Similar to FD rates for equivalent tenure
- Interest compounded quarterly
- Premature closure allowed with penalty of 1% to 2%
- Loan against RD: Up to 90% of deposited amount

RD Eligibility:
- Resident individuals, HUFs, companies, and trusts
- Minor RD can be opened with guardian
- Joint RD allowed (up to 3 holders)

=== DEMAT AND TRADING ACCOUNTS ===

Demat Account:
- Electronic account to hold shares and securities in dematerialized form
- Maintained by depositories NSDL and CDSL
- Annual maintenance charge: Rs. 300 to Rs. 800 per year
- Opening charge: Nil to Rs. 500

3-in-1 Account:
- Combination of savings account, demat account, and trading account
- Seamless fund transfer between accounts
- Recommended for active investors and traders

Life Insurance:

- Endowment plan: Savings + protection combination

General Insurance:

=== BANKING GLOSSARY ===

IMPS: Immediate Payment Service - real-time 24/7 fund transfer up to Rs. 5 lakhs
NEFT: National Electronic Funds Transfer - batch-mode fund transfer, available 24/7
RTGS: Real-Time Gross Settlement - for high-value transfers above Rs. 2 lakhs, minimum Rs. 2 lakhs
UPI: Unified Payments Interface - instant mobile payment system
CIBIL: Credit Information Bureau India Limited - credit bureau maintaining credit scores
EMI: Equated Monthly Installment - fixed monthly payment for loan repayment
NPA: Non-Performing Asset - loan where repayment is overdue beyond 90 days
MCLR: Marginal Cost of Funds-based Lending Rate - benchmark rate for loan pricing
REPO Rate: Rate at which RBI lends money to commercial banks
NOC: No Objection Certificate - issued after loan closure confirming no dues
KYC: Know Your Customer - process to verify customer identity
ECS: Electronic Clearing Service - automatic debit for recurring payments
NACH: National Automated Clearing House - improved version of ECS for bulk transactions
PAN: Permanent Account Number - unique 10-character alphanumeric identifier for tax purposes
TDS: Tax Deducted at Source - tax deducted on interest income above threshold
FD: Fixed Deposit - term deposit with fixed interest for fixed tenure
RD: Recurring Deposit - monthly savings with fixed interest
LTV: Loan-to-Value ratio - percentage of loan against property value
OTP: One-Time Password - single-use code for transaction authentication
ATM: Automated Teller Machine - self-service banking terminal
IFSC: Indian Financial System Code - 11-digit code for bank branch identification in electronic transfers
MICR: Magnetic Ink Character Recognition - 9-digit code on cheques identifying bank and branch
